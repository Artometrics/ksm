Relay was one Expo desk, for iOS and web, holding three jobs: shows, a social queue, and a releases shelf. No GitHub repo. The box path was `/workspace/relay-dep/relay`. The host was [relay-510519.web.app](https://relay-510519.web.app).

## What it was

A small station. The inventory calls it a substantial Expo and Firebase app. The podcast side had RSS, an embeddable player, and a download counter. The social side previewed a calendar for YouTube, X, and Instagram, with connectors off until flags were on. The releases shelf was a UnitedMasters-style board for artwork, splits, and store links. Distribution still happened somewhere else.

## Why it was built

Shows, posts, and releases were landing in three tools. Relay was the attempt to put the queue on one desk. The inventory's recommendation is to archive it as a whole product, not to peel it apart on the way into Dawn.

## How it worked

Expo, with `npm run web` after the emulators. Audio in the starter catalog is a short tone. Firestore (default) existed when the archive was packed. The delete row says that Firestore was empty, the project delete was requested at 8:22 PM PT on 13 Nov 2026, and the host was 404.

## What I learned

Three products in one repo stay three products. The only piece the inventory would even consider for Dawn is the social queue, and it says that queue is not ready to fold in as-is. Connectors were still behind flags.

## Where it went

Retired as a whole product. No part was recorded as moved into Dawn, Bagel, or Pantheon.
